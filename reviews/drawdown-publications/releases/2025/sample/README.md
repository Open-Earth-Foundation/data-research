## Drawdown → GPC mapping

The mapping is **many-to-many** (not one-to-one):

- **Drawdown Sector** → often 2–3 GPC sectors (e.g. "Industry, Materials & Waste" → Stationary Energy + IPPU + Waste)
- **Cluster** refines the GPC subsector (e.g. "Use Waste as a Resource" → III.2 Biological treatment)
- **Solution** overrides resolve ambiguity (e.g. "Alternative Refrigerants" → IV.2 Product use)

See `drawdown-to-gpc-mapping.md` for full guidance.
